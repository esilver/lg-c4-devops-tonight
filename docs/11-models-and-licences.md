# Models, sources and licences

## Read this first

- **The people are synthetic.** The five presenters, the engineer in the hoodie and the faces on background screens were
  generated by an image model from text. None was made from a photograph of a real person, and no likeness is intended.
  If one resembles someone, that is coincidence.
- **The places are synthetic** too: the studio, the data centre, the server aisle, the office and the satellite view.
- **The news is invented.** "DevOps Tonight" is satire. The page says so under its controls and in its credits.
- **The voices are synthetic.** They are stock voices of an open speech model. No real person's voice was recorded,
  cloned or imitated.

## Licences were not checked during the session

The agent downloaded these models and used them for a private demonstration without reading their licence texts. The
licence column below is what it recalls about each, not something it verified. **Check each model card before reusing
any image or file from this repository for anything beyond looking at it.** Some of these licences restrict commercial
use or particular kinds of use, and licence terms change.

its bundled Python wheel were deliberately not downloaded, and neither were its face detection and landmark models.
